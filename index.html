How long does a website take?

A standard business website usually takes 1–3 weeks depending on pages and features. Apps and custom software are planned in milestones — you'll get a clear timeline before we start.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We modernise the design, speed it up, make it fully mobile-friendly and keep your content and SEO intact.

Do you provide domain and hosting?

We help you buy and set up your domain, hosting and business email so everything is ready to go live.

Will I be able to update content myself?

If you need it, we add a simple admin panel so you can update text, images and products without touching code.

What about support after launch?

We stay with you after launch — fixes, updates and new features are just a WhatsApp message away.
